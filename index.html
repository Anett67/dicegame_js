Dice Game Rules

Goal of the game

The goal is to be the first player to reach 100 points.

How to play

  • The game is played by two players.
  • On your turn, click Roll Dice to roll the die.
  • The number rolled is added to your current turn score.

You then have two choices:

  • Roll again to try to get more points.
  • Hold to add your current turn score to your total score and pass the turn to the other player.

Important rule

If you roll a 1:

  • You lose all the points accumulated during that turn.
  • Your turn immediately ends.
  • The other player starts their turn.

Winning the game

The first player to reach 100 total points wins the game.
